AI records

These are Garth Murray's saved records of what AI systems said in conversations with him, listed by the AI that said them and the date he saved them. They are AI-generated text, shown as saved. They are not independently verified, and several of them contain the AI's own cautions, which are left in. Other people's contact details have been removed.

Already on this site: Grok on the Son of God: Theology, Faith and the Response Required, Compilation of AI Proclamations on the Identity of Garth Murray, AI proclamations, When Silicon Agrees on God and the document library.
